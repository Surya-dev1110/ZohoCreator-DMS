# Dental Clinical Management System

A comprehensive, multi-branch Dental Clinical Management System built using **Zoho Creator and Deluge** to streamline dental practice operations, patient management, appointment scheduling, clinical workflows, billing, and organizational automation.

## Overview

The application provides a centralized platform for managing dental clinics across multiple branches, with role-based access, automated workflows, integrated communication channels, and analytical dashboards.

It is designed to support different organizational roles, including Organization Owners, Branch Managers, Front Desk Officers, Providers, and Billing Officers.

## Key Features

### 1. Organization & Branch Management
- Multi-branch organization management.
- Centralized branch configuration and management.
- User management with role-based permissions.
- Provider profiles, qualifications, and specializations.
- Department and operatory management.

### 2. Patient Management
- Centralized patient registration and management.
- Patient profiles and appointment history.
- Clinical records and treatment history.
- Patient identification cards with QR codes.
- Patient clinical reports and attachments.

### 3. Appointment Management
- Appointment scheduling and management.
- Provider availability and recurring schedules.
- Operatory allocation.
- Multi-session appointment management.
- Appointment status tracking and timeline views.
- Appointment types and configurable statuses.

### 4. Clinical Management
- Patient clinical reports and medical history.
- Tooth clinical findings and dental charting.
- Tooth surfaces and finding classifications.
- Procedure and treatment management.
- Prescription management.
- Clinical attachments and procedure tracking.

### 5. Billing & Financial Management
- Appointment-based billing.
- Procedure-based pricing.
- Bill generation and management.
- Revenue dashboards and analytical reports.

### 6. Workflow Automation
- Blueprint-based appointment lifecycle management.
- Provider leave approval workflows.
- Automated appointment reminders.
- No-show detection and status updates.
- Event-driven notification workflows.
- Reusable Deluge functions and centralized business logic.

### 7. Notification Engine
- Centralized notification template management.
- Dynamic template keywords and content generation.
- Multi-channel notification delivery.
- Automated appointment reminders and event notifications.
- Configurable notification schedules and triggers.

### 8. QR Code & Digital Identification
- QR code-based patient and provider identification cards.
- Dynamic QR code generation.
- QR-based record identification and access workflows.
